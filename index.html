Packing checklist

  1. Sort parts into numbered bags using the bag labels (each label pictures its parts).
  2. Print the booklet double-sided, short-edge flip, and staple or saddle-stitch it.
  3. Print the box front and back, glue to a plain box (or a sleeve) sized to the page.
  4. Add the booklet, the bags, and a spare-parts bag. Seal and wrap.
